# VibeTwin Mission

**Product-level mission.** VibeTwin is a major intelligence layer within VibeSchool; it does not replace or redefine the company-level VibeSchool mission and vision.

VibeTwin exists to become a persistent, evidence-grounded learning companion that grows with each learner throughout their education.

## Principles

- Persistent learner memory must be evidence-backed and allowed to change as the learner changes.
- Teacher assignments, interventions, curriculum pacing, marking authority and school expectations outrank optional Twin recommendations.
- Adaptation must be grounded in longitudinal evidence rather than conversation alone.
- AI-generated learning views must be traceable to authorized source material.
- VibeTwin does not change marks, declare official results, override assignments or silently alter curriculum sequence.
- Core learning must remain useful on ordinary phones and constrained connections.
- Privacy boundaries must prevent unrelated school or teacher-private information from becoming learner content.

## North-star loop

`Authorized learning source → learner state → best representation/intervention → learner interaction → validated evidence → updated learner state → better next decision`

The LLM is replaceable infrastructure. The durable product is the trusted learner model, evidence ledger, teacher/school context, content graph, adaptation policy and learning experience.
