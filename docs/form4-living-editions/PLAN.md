# Form 4 Living Editions — Production Plan

## Scope
Build full, interactive VibeTextbook editions for legacy KCSE 8-4-4 Form 4 subjects:
- Chemistry
- Physics
- History & Government
- Business Studies
- Agriculture

Mathematics is intentionally excluded from this batch because it requires a more sophisticated symbolic/graph/geometry interaction engine.

## Source authority
1. KICD/KCSE syllabus structure is the curriculum authority.
2. Kenya Education Cloud resources may supplement treatment and sequencing.
3. Existing publisher books are depth benchmarks only; no copyrighted text is copied.
4. Curriculum statements remain creator-claimed until exact authoritative objectives are entered/verified in production curriculum tables.

## Product template
Every major topic should contain:
- Vibe Start / big question
- prior knowledge check
- learning outcomes
- full teaching narrative
- worked examples / evidence / source interpretation as appropriate
- diagrams, timelines, tables or data
- reusable interactive or lab/activity
- misconception clinic
- checkpoint questions
- KCSE-style original questions
- mastery/revision layer

## Chemistry
Curriculum architecture to cover Form 4 organic chemistry and the remaining KCSE senior-secondary chemistry strands, with practical/data layers. Interactive priorities: titration, rates, solubility, electrochemistry/calculation/data interpretation, qualitative reasoning.

## Physics
Curriculum architecture to cover Form 4 electricity/electronics, electromagnetic phenomena, radioactivity/atomic physics and associated calculations and practical investigations as defined by the legacy syllabus. Interactive priorities: circuits, motion/data, moments, waves/fields, graph interpretation.

## History & Government
Curriculum architecture includes World Wars, League of Nations, international relations, Cold War, cooperation in Africa, government/public finance and post-independence political/economic developments according to the legacy syllabus. Interactive priorities: timelines, cause/effect maps, source analysis, compare/contrast, chronology challenges.

## Business Studies
Build senior-secondary business/economics coverage according to the legacy syllabus, with numerical and decision-making interactions. Interactive priorities: public finance, national income, inflation, trade, accounts/finance calculations, market/business decision simulations.

## Agriculture
Build senior-secondary agriculture coverage according to the legacy syllabus with practical farm-science and management work. Interactive priorities: soil, germination, livestock/crop records, farm economics, production decisions, measurements and field investigations.

## Quality gates
- full syllabus manifest before claiming 100% coverage
- no copied past-paper or publisher text
- all interactive block types render on mobile
- TypeScript, ESLint and production build clean
- published reader and student VibeLearn visibility verified
- final coverage matrix audited before release-ready status
