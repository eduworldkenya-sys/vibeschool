## Authority and provenance

This production corpus is deliberately bound to the legacy Kenya Institute of Education (KIE) **Secondary Education Syllabus Volume Two (2002), Agriculture, Form Four**, not the Grade 10 CBE Agriculture curriculum.

The official Form Four sequence transcribed for this seed is:

1. `27.0.0` Livestock Production V (Poultry)
2. `28.0.0` Livestock Production VI (Cattle)
3. `29.0.0` Farm Power and Machinery
4. `30.0.0` Agricultural Economics III (Production Economics)
5. `31.0.0` Agricultural Economics IV (Farm Accounts)
6. `32.0.0` Agricultural Economics V (Agricultural Marketing and Organisations)
7. `33.0.0` Agroforestry

The institutional authority is KIE/KICD. The currently discoverable online legacy copy used for line-by-line transcription is a mirror of the KIE 2002 syllabus because the exact legacy syllabus PDF was not recoverable from the current KICD public index during this run. Consequently, authority outcomes remain `draft` and chapter alignment remains `creator_claimed`; no `verified_by`, teacher approval, human curriculum approval or release-authorisation identity is fabricated.

## Canonical architecture

The seed reuses the canonical History/Business production architecture:

- existing global `Agriculture` subject;
- `public.curriculum` for seven Form IV authority units;
- `public.curriculum_learning_outcomes` for 55 source-labelled objectives;
- the existing canonical `public.vibe_publications` Agriculture publication, upgraded in place rather than duplicated;
- `public.vibe_chapters` for seven ordered textbook chapters;
- canonical public reader route `/read/textbook/[publicationId]/[chapterId]`;
- learning-layer metadata: `orient`, `comprehend`, `apply`, `connect`, `extend`;
- Teacher OS and Student OS derivative blocks inside every chapter;
- subject-neutral `LearningLoopArticle` reader already present on canonical `main`.

The four pre-existing Agriculture draft shells were only 107–129 words and did not correspond to the official Form Four unit structure. They were removed by exact ID with `draft` and `<200 word` guards before replacement. No unrelated publication or chapter was deleted.

## Learning-system evidence

Every chapter contains, where relevant:

- syllabus focus and learner orientation;
- concept and technical explanations;
- Kenyan farming context;
- practical or data/calculation activity;
- explicit machinery/animal/food-safety boundaries;
- diagnostic checkpoint;
- misconception-oriented learning logic;
- original KCSE-style practice;
- technical visual-learning specification;
- Teacher OS derivative activity;
- Student OS derivative activity.

Volatile facts such as vaccine schedules, veterinary drug withdrawal periods, pesticide concentrations, current prices and current institutional mandates are not encoded as timeless syllabus facts.
