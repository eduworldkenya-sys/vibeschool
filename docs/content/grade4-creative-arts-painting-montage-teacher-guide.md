# Grade 4 Creative Arts — 1.3 Painting and Montage

## Learning intentions

Learners identify primary and secondary paint colours, mix secondary colours, vary colour value to paint a rounded object, assemble a ball-games montage using overlap, and appreciate painting and montage as ways of communicating ideas.

## Preparation and safe practice

Provide red, yellow, blue, white and black school paints, brushes, rinse water, palettes, paper, old magazines or original drawings, child-safe scissors, glue, aprons and a drying area. Avoid requiring purchased supplies; use locally available alternatives. Never taste paints; wash hands after use. Supervise cutting and prevent spills. Show each step before learners try it. Use large-print colour labels, tactile cutouts and spoken descriptions for accessibility; do not rely on colour alone.

## Instruction and differentiation

Start each lesson with an observable example, model the action, guide a short pair practice, allow independent work, then invite explanation. For learners needing support, supply large pre-drawn shapes, colour-name cards, pre-cut paper and one step at a time. For extension, ask learners to create a four-step value strip or show action with deliberate overlap. Assess process and explanation as well as the finished product. A learner who cannot distinguish some colours visually may classify labelled paint containers and explain the mixing procedure.

## Practical assessment

A. Primary and secondary swatch chart. B. Value strip with original colour, tints and shades. C. Painted ball with visible tonal variation. D. Ball-games montage with deliberate overlap. Use formative feedback throughout. Suggested painting rubric /20: colour handling 5, tonal variation 5, shape/observation 5, care/explanation 5. Montage rubric /20: identifiable ball-game story 5, arrangement/overlap 5, craftsmanship 5, reflection 5. Rubrics are VibeSchool-original, not claimed as official KICD scoring.

## Reflection and follow-up

Ask: Which mixture surprised you? What helped your ball look round? Why did you overlap pictures? What does your montage communicate? What would you improve? Provide a second try for weak mixing or poor overlap, and display improved work without ranking children publicly.
