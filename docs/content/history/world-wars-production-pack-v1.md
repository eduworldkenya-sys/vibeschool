## Unit blueprints

### H4-WW-01 First World War — Causes
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(a).
Core teaching sequence: pre-1914 Europe; nationalism; imperial/economic rivalry; militarism and arms race; alliance systems; Balkan tensions; Sarajevo assassination; July Crisis; distinction between structural causes and immediate trigger; causal synthesis.
Visual briefs: Europe alliance map c.1914; Balkans locator; June–August 1914 escalation timeline; causal web.
Exam focus: explain causes; distinguish trigger from underlying causes; connect alliance system to escalation.
Status: draft teaching content already seeded separately; must pass all gates.

### H4-WW-02 First World War — Course
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(b).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-01.
Core sequence: outbreak and belligerents; Western Front; Eastern Front; war at sea; wider/global theatres and colonial participation; trench warfare and military technology; major turning points; United States entry; Russia's withdrawal; 1918 offensives; armistice and reasons the Allies prevailed.
Timeline anchors: 1914 outbreak; 1915–16 expansion/attrition; 1917 US entry and Russian revolution/withdrawal; 1918 armistice.
Visual briefs: fronts map; trench cross-section; global theatre map; 1914–18 timeline.
Misconception targets: war was fought only in France; US fought from 1914; armistice equals peace treaty.
Exam focus: describe course chronologically; explain turning points/reasons for Allied victory where question demands explanation.

### H4-WW-03 First World War — Results
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(c).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-01/02.
Core sequence: human and social costs; economic destruction/debt; political change and collapsed empires; territorial changes; peace settlement and Treaty of Versailles context; mandates/new states; international cooperation and League connection; colonial/African consequences; conditions contributing to later instability.
Visual briefs: Europe before/after map; consequence tree; peace-settlement timeline.
Misconception targets: Treaty of Versailles ended fighting on 11 Nov 1918; all consequences were immediate; League and UN are the same body.
Exam focus: discuss results across political, economic, social and international dimensions.

### H4-WW-04 League of Nations — Formation
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(d).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-03.
Core sequence: destruction of WWI; search for collective security; Wilson/Fourteen Points context; Paris Peace Conference/Covenant context; aims of peace, cooperation, disarmament and dispute settlement; membership context.
Visual briefs: WWI → peace settlement → League flow; Geneva locator; formation timeline.
Misconception targets: League created before WWI ended; Wilson alone created it; US membership assumed.
Exam focus: explain reasons for formation, not merely list aims.

### H4-WW-05 League of Nations — Organization
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(e).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-04.
Core sequence: Assembly; Council; Secretariat; Permanent Court of International Justice; International Labour Organization and relevant commissions/agencies; membership/decision processes; how organs were intended to work together.
Visual briefs: organization chart; decision pathway.
Misconception targets: confusing Assembly and Council; treating all associated bodies as identical in legal status; confusing League institutions with UN institutions.
Exam focus: describe organization and functions accurately.

### H4-WW-06 League of Nations — Performance
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(f).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-04/05.
Core sequence: define performance as achievements + failures + constraints; selected dispute settlements/humanitarian work; refugees/health/labour cooperation where supported; disarmament difficulties; Manchurian crisis; Abyssinian crisis; membership/absence of major powers; unanimity and enforcement weaknesses; appeasement/aggression context; balanced judgement.
Visual briefs: achievements-vs-failures matrix; crisis timeline; collective-security decision chain.
Misconception targets: League achieved nothing; failure had one cause; League possessed its own standing army.
Exam focus: analyse performance using evidence and balanced judgement.

### H4-WW-07 Second World War — Causes
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(a).
Prerequisites: WWI results + League performance.
Core sequence: weaknesses/resentments from post-WWI settlement; rise of aggressive dictatorships and expansionism; economic crisis context; failure of collective security/League; appeasement; rearmament and alliance/diplomatic developments; specific acts of aggression; Nazi-Soviet Pact context; invasion of Poland as immediate outbreak trigger in Europe; causal synthesis.
Visual briefs: Europe 1939; aggression timeline; cause web.
Misconception targets: one-cause explanation; Great Depression alone caused war; appeasement equals alliance with Hitler.
Exam focus: explain causes and connect long-term conditions to immediate outbreak.

### H4-WW-08 Second World War — Course
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(b).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-07.
Core sequence: invasion of Poland/outbreak; early Axis victories and fall of France; Battle of Britain; Mediterranean/North Africa; Operation Barbarossa; Pearl Harbor and US entry; global/Pacific war; major turning points; Allied advances; D-Day/liberation; Germany's surrender; Pacific end and Japan's surrender.
Visual briefs: Europe campaigns; North Africa; Eastern Front; Pacific theatre; 1939–45 global timeline.
Misconception targets: Pearl Harbor started the European war; D-Day ended WWII immediately; Germany and Japan surrendered together.
Exam focus: describe course in sequence and explain turning points only when command word requires it.

### H4-WW-09 Second World War — Results
Objective mapping: 24.1.0(c).
Prerequisites: H4-WW-07/08.
Core sequence: casualties/destruction/displacement; Holocaust and war-crimes context handled accurately and age-appropriately; economic consequences/reconstruction; weakening of European imperial powers; US/USSR superpower emergence and Cold War context; United Nations formation; territorial/political changes; decolonization/nationalism implications including Africa; technological/military consequences including atomic age; international justice/human-rights developments where properly sourced.
Visual briefs: consequence map; 1945 geopolitical map; WWI-v-WWII comparison table.
Misconception targets: UN and League identical; WWII alone caused African independence; atomic bombs were used in Europe.
Exam focus: discuss results across categories and show historical significance.

## Question and examination contract
Question objects require: unit_id; objective_code; command_word; question_text; marks; difficulty; knowledge/skill target; expected answer type; marking points; acceptable alternatives; common errors; model answer; source requirement if source-based; reviewer status.

Required mix per completed unit: 5 retrieval checks; 3 understanding questions; 2 causal/chronological reasoning questions; 2 KCSE-style structured questions; 1 higher-order mastery task. Where historically appropriate add one evidence/source-analysis task. Questions must not test untaught material.

Command-word discipline:
- Name/Identify/State/Give: concise valid points; no forced explanation.
- Outline: key features/sequence with limited elaboration.
- Describe: coherent account of what/how/sequence.
- Explain: point + historical development + explicit causal link.
- Discuss: multiple developed dimensions, evidence and balanced treatment where appropriate.
- Analyse: break into components/relationships and reach supported judgement where appropriate.

## Marking-scheme contract
Every scored item states total marks, allocation logic, acceptable points, required development, alternative valid responses, maximum credit and common non-credit responses. Never manufacture a KNEC marking scheme; label VibeSchool schemes as curriculum-aligned practice marking guidance unless derived from an authorized past-paper scheme.

Claim record fields: claim_id; unit_id; claim_text; claim_type; source_title; source_locator; source_url_or_repo_ref; verification_status; checked_by; checked_at; notes.
