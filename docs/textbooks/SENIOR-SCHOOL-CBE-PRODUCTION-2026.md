# Senior School CBE Living Editions — 2026 production authority

Status: active production authority for current Kenya Senior School textbook development.

## Curriculum truth

Current Kenyan Senior School is Competency-Based Education (CBE), Grades 10–12. The current KICD Grade 10 curriculum-design collection includes Agriculture, Business Studies, Chemistry, Physics and History & Citizenship. Legacy Form 4 / KCSE 8-4-4 publications remain a separate product line and must never be relabelled as current CBE content.

## Current subject identities

- Agriculture — Grade 10 CBE
- Business Studies — Grade 10 CBE
- Chemistry — Grade 10 CBE
- Physics — Grade 10 CBE
- History & Citizenship — Grade 10 CBE

Future editions extend the same line to Grade 11 and Grade 12 using the then-current KICD designs.

## Source hierarchy

1. Current KICD curriculum designs are authoritative for scope, strands, sub-strands, outcomes, suggested experiences, competencies, values, PCIs and assessment guidance.
2. Current KNEC Senior School assessment circulars/frameworks govern assessment treatment.
3. Current KICD approved-materials and submission/evaluation documents define publisher-quality expectations.
4. Kenya Education Cloud and reputable primary/educational sources may enrich explanations and contemporary examples, but may not override KICD curriculum requirements.
5. Publisher books are benchmarking references only. No copyrighted wording, diagrams, questions or answer keys may be copied.

## Product architecture

Every curriculum requirement must trace to authored textbook content and, where appropriate:

- prior-knowledge activation
- learner-facing explanation
- worked example / evidence interpretation
- Kenyan and contemporary context
- practical investigation / source inquiry / decision task
- VibeLab or other executable interaction
- competency and value application
- misconception clinic
- checkpoint assessment
- end-of-strand assessment
- teacher derivation support
- Twin evidence hook

## Definition of complete

A book is not complete because it has many pages or chapters. It is complete only when every current KICD curriculum requirement has a traceable treatment, assessment and QA state, all required practical/inquiry experiences are represented safely, content is editorially correct, mobile rendering works, and learner/teacher flows have been verified.

## Legacy separation

Existing drafts titled `Vibe <Subject> Form 4` remain `KCSE 8-4-4` legacy editions. They may continue to serve current legacy KCSE candidates while that examination pathway exists, but they are not the current Senior School CBE editions.
