# VibeTutor to-do (not shown on the front end)

## Needed before anything can be listed (backend phase)
- Tutor: phone number, kept private until a request is agreed
- Tutor: holiday availability (days and times)
- Tutor: consent to public listing
- Learner or guardian: name or nickname, guardian phone number (private)
- Learner or guardian: mode (online, in person, either), budget in KSh per hour, schedule including holiday dates
- Guardian consent for learners under 18
- Sign-in with a one-time code
- Account deletion and data-retention rules (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019), before launch

## Needed later
- Tutor bio, profile photo, qualifications or experience
- Background or good-conduct check and identity verification for tutors
- In-app messaging, with phone numbers hidden until a booking is agreed
- Reporting and blocking
- Reviews after sessions
- Cancellation and no-show terms
- Payment rules: whether VibeTutor takes a fee, and how payment works

## Search filters (planned)
- Subject
- Level or class (replaces age; age mostly duplicates class)
- Area
- Locality (a finer area: estate or town within an area)
- Mode: online, in person, either
- Time: morning, afternoon, evening, weekend, holiday days
- Rate in KSh per hour
