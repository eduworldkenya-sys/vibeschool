# VibeSchool Business Operating Doctrine

Status: **CANONICAL BUSINESS STRATEGY — FOUNDER READABLE**  
Date: 2026-08-23  
Scope: product, growth, studio, distribution, pricing, revenue, sponsorship, school conversion and HQ measurement.

This doctrine sits below the locked Mission/Vision constitution. It does not replace the product north star or authority model. It translates them into a commercial operating model that HQ can measure.

## What VibeSchool sells

VibeSchool sells **continuous educational progress**, not access to files, books or AI in isolation.

The commercial promise is that a learner can return every learning day and move through a connected loop:

**Learn → Practise → Get feedback → Understand weakness → Improve → Know what to do next.**

The same educational state supports four primary customer products:

1. **Student** — daily learning, revision, practice, feedback, mastery and next actions.
2. **Teacher** — less preparation/administration work, stronger teaching evidence and class intelligence.
3. **Parent/Family** — clear evidence of whether a child is learning, improving and where support is needed.
4. **School** — an operating system for teaching, learning, evidence, intervention and institutional visibility.

Studio, VibePress, VibeLab and AI are production/intelligence capabilities inside that value system. They are not the definition of the company by themselves.

## Recurring-customer philosophy

Do not manufacture retention through friction or artificial lock-in. Build a product that solves a problem that naturally occurs again tomorrow.

Education already supplies the recurrence: another lesson, question, difficult concept, homework task, assessment, weakness to repair and next learning action.

The retention loop is:

**Need → useful learning action → evidence → visible progress → next action → return.**

## Wallet and pricing philosophy

The wallet exists to reduce payment friction, not to turn every click into a toll gate. A parent can fund several learning days at once while VibeSchool accounts for usage internally. Pricing should remain experimentally governed until real retention, payment-cost and AI-cost evidence is available.

> Keep learner pricing highly accessible; grow company value through scale, retention, school contracts, teacher productivity, premium learning products, sponsorship and distribution rather than extracting a high fee from each family.

Revenue is not profit. HQ must track payment costs, inference cost, storage/bandwidth, support, commissions, taxes and other direct costs so each learning day has positive unit economics.

## Access philosophy

Do not create a deliberately inferior education product for a learner because the family cannot pay. Use a strong free baseline plus sponsored learning credits, school licences, CSR programmes, NGO/institutional programmes, scholarships and approved sponsor-funded learning periods. Educational quality and learner safety remain governed by the same standards.

## Growth and distribution doctrine

Growth is a measured distribution system, not a vanity-metrics department.

Primary starter channels:

1. **Teachers** — one teacher can activate a whole class.
2. **TikTok/short-form educational challenges** — useful content creates attention and routes learners to a specific diagnostic or learning action.
3. **School-gate distribution** — school-specific QR/campaign codes convert physical reach into measurable acquisition.
4. **Student referrals/ambassadors** — learners invite peers around useful challenges and study activity.
5. **Creators** — measured by activated and paying learners, not views alone.
6. **Field agents** — compensated on verified outcomes, not on flyers claimed as distributed.
7. **Schools** — bottom-up student/teacher adoption matures into institutional contracts.

Canonical acquisition funnel:

**Touch → Visit/Scan → Signup → First learning action → Day-2 return → First wallet deposit/payment → Repeat payment → Retained learner.**

Every campaign must carry attribution wherever technically and legally appropriate.

## School conversion doctrine

The strategic unit is not simply registered users. HQ should measure **Paying Active Learners per Active School (PAL/School)** alongside WAU, retention and revenue.

School progression:

**Target → Contacted → Learners using → Teacher champion → Active school → Paying users → Institutional prospect → School customer.**

## Studio doctrine

Studio is VibeSchool's production system. Studio creates and governs curriculum learning content, VibePress publications, VibeLab experiences, assessment media, lesson/revision videos, TikTok/Shorts educational creatives, campaign assets, flyers/QR creatives, sponsor-safe creatives and creator submissions.

Operating loop:

**Studio creates → Growth distributes → Product delivers value → Finance records economics → Intelligence identifies what works → Studio improves the next production cycle.**

Studio is accountable for quality, throughput, provenance and release readiness. Growth is accountable for distribution and attributable outcomes.

## Product doctrine

Product answers: **Are people getting real value after they enter VibeSchool?**

Core product metrics should include WAU by role, learning days per active learner, activation, Day-2 / Week-1 / Week-2 / 30-day retention, lesson/practice completion, valid mastery progression, wallet conversion, repeat payment, feature adoption, failure points and PAL/School.

Never report inferred educational improvement as measured mastery without appropriate evidence.

## Marketing/Growth doctrine

Marketing answers: **How did people discover and enter VibeSchool, and which sources create retained paying learners?**

Views, impressions and registrations are leading indicators; retained learning and economically sustainable conversion are stronger business outcomes.

## Revenue engines

1. learner learning-day/wallet access;
2. premium revision, mastery, mock and holiday products;
3. teacher professional tools;
4. school/institutional licences;
5. sponsored learning credits and CSR programmes;
6. publishing/content sales and future marketplace/distribution economics;
7. carefully governed advertising/sponsorship where appropriate.

Advertising must not depend on exposing sensitive learner profiles. Child safety, privacy, educational appropriateness and owner governance are mandatory.

## Competitive moat

Do not define the moat as CBC alignment, AI chat, books or UI. Those can be copied.

The deeper moat is **educational continuity**: a trusted, permissioned record of curriculum position, learning activity, evidence, strengths, gaps, interventions and next actions connected across learner, teacher, family and school workflows.

Distribution compounds the moat. When VibeSchool owns a trusted relationship with learners, teachers and schools, VibePress, VibeLab and future partners gain immediate distribution.

## HQ operating loop

**Business doctrine defines intent → Product proves value → Studio creates → Growth distributes → Finance measures economics → Intelligence interprets evidence → Founder Control Room surfaces decisions.**

The doctrine is readable; the metrics are executable evidence. Neither should silently replace the other.

## Founder metrics

The founder should be able to see, at minimum:

- WAU and WAU by role;
- paying active learners;
- active schools;
- PAL/School;
- wallet/revenue trend;
- activation and retention;
- funnel conversion by channel;
- CAC where spend is known;
- revenue by channel;
- school pipeline stages;
- Studio production/release throughput;
- unit-cost/inference-cost trend;
- sponsor-funded learner reach;
- unresolved growth/product risks.

Unknown metrics must display **NOT INSTRUMENTED** or equivalent rather than fabricated estimates.

## Safety and authority

Growth, Studio and AI workers may analyse, draft and recommend. They do not gain automatic authority to spend money, pay commissions, publish consequential content, message children, weaken consent, activate Worker runtime or alter pricing without the required governance and owner controls.
