# VibeSchool Mission, Vision & Product North Star — LOCKED

Status: **LOCKED STRATEGIC CONSTITUTION**  
Date: 2026-08-17  
Scope: product, public website, engineering, sales, content, AI, growth and future platform decisions.

This document exists to prevent VibeSchool from drifting into a generic school ERP, a feature-count competitor, an AI wrapper, or a marketing site that promises more than the product can prove.

## Mission

> **Build the Education Operating System that turns curriculum into coordinated teaching, teaching into evidence, evidence into understanding, and understanding into better decisions for every learner.**

The mission is operational, not decorative. Every major product surface should strengthen at least one link in that chain.

## Vision

> **Every learner known. Every lesson connected. Every outcome visible.**

VibeSchool's long-term vision is a continuous educational state that can remain useful across teaching, learning, family support, school leadership and future progression without collapsing those people into one role or one all-access account.

## Product North-Star Loop

**KNOW → PLAN → TEACH → LEARN → PROVE → ADAPT → REPORT → PROGRESS**

Interpretation:

1. **KNOW** — establish trusted curriculum, learner, class, school and relationship context.
2. **PLAN** — translate curriculum intent into schemes, lessons and appropriate learning activity.
3. **TEACH** — record the real teaching occurrence, not only the document that was prepared beforehand.
4. **LEARN** — connect the learner to meaningful resources, tasks, practice and participation.
5. **PROVE** — capture evidence through work, observation, submission and assessment.
6. **ADAPT** — use evidence to identify strengths, gaps, intervention needs and uncertainty.
7. **REPORT** — communicate the right educational context to the right person.
8. **PROGRESS** — carry useful learner state forward into the next lesson, term, pathway and decision.

## Canonical Product Sequence

**Curriculum → Learning outcomes → Scheme → Lesson plan → Teaching occurrence → Evidence → Assessment → Learner outcome/mastery → Intervention/reflection → Next learning action**

This sequence is the product backbone. New features should connect to it rather than forming parallel systems.

## Teacher-First Entry Strategy

VibeSchool remains **teacher-first in operational adoption**.

Teachers are the practical entry point because teaching is where curriculum becomes real learner activity and evidence. Teacher-first does **not** mean teacher-only. The learner educational state is the core product asset, while learners, families and schools receive role-appropriate experiences around that state.

A useful shorthand is:

**Teacher teaches → learner acts → evidence forms → understanding improves → family understands → school sees → next action becomes clearer.**

## Authority Model

VibeSchool distinguishes between seven educational authorities:

- Curriculum
- Teaching
- Learning
- Assessment
- Competency / mastery
- School / institutional authority
- Intelligence

**AI is not an authority.** AI may explain, draft, organise, recommend or assist, but trusted curriculum, authorised records, learner evidence and accountable human decisions remain the source of truth.

## Non-Negotiable Product Principles

1. **Design systems, not isolated pages.**
2. **Do not optimise for feature count.** A feature that does not strengthen the educational loop must justify its complexity.
3. **The teacher remains professionally responsible.**
4. **AI supports teaching and learning; it does not fabricate reality.**
5. **Every curriculum claim must remain curriculum-aligned and evidence-aware.**
6. **Every learner insight must be traceable to appropriate evidence or explicitly marked as uncertain/inferred.**
7. **Role and relationship boundaries are product features, not implementation details.**
8. **Every consequential workflow must work on an ordinary Android phone.**
9. **Offline and printable workflows are first-class where the educational context requires them.**
10. **Parents receive clarity, not educational jargon or unrestricted access.**
11. **Do not create parallel architectures to solve local problems.** Extend the canonical system.
12. **Investigate before fixing.** Diagnose root cause and preserve invariants.
13. **Every production change requires evidence and verification.**
14. **Never sacrifice authorization, evidence integrity, learner trust or truthful claims for growth.**

## Public Website Mission

The website must make VibeSchool understandable and useful **before** asking a visitor to believe marketing claims.

The public experience should repeatedly demonstrate:

**what should happen → what actually happens → what becomes known → what the right person can do next.**

The website therefore competes through:

- educational intelligence;
- useful interactive diagnostics;
- connected-system explanations;
- role-specific journeys;
- trustworthy boundaries;
- public learning value;
- transparent capability status;
- evidence-backed claims;
- clear school conversion.

Screenshots are supportive proof, not the strategy.

## Competitive Rule

Do not compete by saying “VibeSchool has more modules.”

Do not compete only on price.

Do not copy another Kenyan product's information architecture simply because it currently sells well.

Use competitor research to classify discoveries:

- **PARITY** — necessary table stakes.
- **BEAT** — strategically important capability worth outperforming.
- **IGNORE** — complexity that does not strengthen the mission.
- **OWN** — territory VibeSchool can uniquely make difficult to replicate.

Current OWN territory:

- curriculum-to-evidence continuity;
- continuous learner educational state;
- intervention-effect learning;
- evidence-grounded learner intelligence;
- parent understanding connected to real learning state;
- Pathways connected to learner progression;
- network economics tied to genuine educational participation.

## Commercial Principle

Pricing exists to expand genuine educational participation, not to define the brand as cheap software.

Network sponsorship, future memberships and institutional pricing must reward legitimate adoption without making raw logins or fabricated activity economically valuable.

## Decision Hierarchy

When product, engineering, marketing or commercial options conflict, use this order:

1. learner safety and authorization;
2. evidence integrity and educational truth;
3. mission / canonical learning loop;
4. teacher usability and real classroom workflow;
5. learner usefulness and progression;
6. family clarity;
7. institutional visibility and operations;
8. commercial growth;
9. visual novelty / feature count.

## Change Control

This file is intentionally marked **LOCKED**.

A future change to the Mission, Vision, North-Star Loop, Canonical Product Sequence or Decision Hierarchy must be explicit, documented and reviewed as a strategic change. It must not happen indirectly through a homepage rewrite, competitor-copy exercise, isolated feature request or migration.
