## Governing model

> **VibeSchool Pathways is a free, trusted education decision/navigation and customer-acquisition layer that converts authoritative Kenyan education evidence into understandable, action-first guidance.**

**ASK → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → PERSONALIZE → ACT → HELP WHEN NEEDED → REMEMBER/CONTINUE.**

It is not merely a quiz, chatbot, SEO project, school directory or marketplace.

## Non-negotiable invariants

- Free core; value before primary sign-in.
- Authentication is for continuity/persistence/deeper authorized personalization.
- Anonymous Quick Check stays device-local until explicit Save under the current P0 contract.
- Canonical onboarding dominates continuation.
- No second learner identity, school identity, auth router, sitemap authority or Pathways truth brain.
- Existing school-linked learner signup safeguards are not weakened for conversion.
- Parent/teacher support cannot overwrite learner-owned Pathway Passport state.
- Formal assessments and mastery/task recommendations remain separate domains.
- Twin may explain Pathways later but cannot invent pathway eligibility or school offerings.
- Public facts require provenance; school pathway/combination filtering requires verified offering evidence.
- Missing offering evidence means “not yet verified”, not “does not exist”.
- Official fact, learner evidence and VibeSchool guidance remain distinguishable.
- Eligibility, suitability and aspiration remain distinct.
- No fake recommendation precision.
- Commercial relationships never alter educational truth/ranking.
- Public SEO/AI surfaces never expose private learner data.
- #1 search/AI discoverability is a measured objective, not a guarantee.
- Documentation is not implementation; implementation is not certification.
