# VibeSchool Pathways — Customer Acquisition Strategy

**Status:** Product strategy baseline  
**Branch:** `agent/pathways-customer-acquisition-strategy`  
**Scope:** Discovery, acquisition, activation, retention and future assistance marketplace  
**Principle:** Free discovery. Sign in to continue. Pay only for optional additional services.

## 1. Strategic Decision

VibeSchool Pathways will be treated first as a **customer-acquisition and activation engine**, not as a paywall product.

Core pathway discovery remains free. A visitor should be able to experience meaningful value before being asked to create an account. At the point where the pathway becomes personally useful and worth preserving, VibeSchool asks the learner or parent to sign in or create a free account.

The account is not payment for the pathway. It converts an anonymous discovery session into a persistent VibeSchool relationship that can support the learner over time.

VibeSchool may later monetize optional human assistance, convenience and professional services around the free pathway. Access to basic opportunity discovery must not depend on payment.

## 2. Vision

**Every learner in Kenya should be able to discover where they can go, understand how to get there, and access the people and opportunities that can help them succeed, regardless of their ability to pay.**

VibeSchool Pathways should become the free gateway into that journey: helping learners and parents understand pathways, subjects, careers, schools and next steps, then connecting them to the wider VibeSchool ecosystem when deeper support is useful.

## 3. Mission

**Make pathway discovery free, useful and accessible, then turn every pathway into an actionable, persistent learning journey.**

VibeSchool will:

- keep core pathway discovery and guidance free;
- allow visitors to experience meaningful pathway value before authentication;
- trigger free sign-in at a genuine value moment rather than at the entrance;
- preserve pathway results and next steps in the learner's VibeSchool account;
- connect pathway guidance to learning, progress, teachers, parents and schools;
- enable optional paid professional assistance without restricting the free core;
- create new earning opportunities for verified teachers where families voluntarily request human assistance;
- help schools connect with learners whose interests and pathways are relevant to their programmes;
- measure the complete acquisition funnel so product decisions are evidence-led.

## 4. Product Promise

> **Discover for free. Sign in to continue. Get help when you need it. Pay only when you choose additional services.**

## 5. Who We Acquire

Pathways should serve four connected audiences, with the learner and parent as the primary acquisition pair.

### Primary

1. **Learners** — need direction, confidence, subject/pathway clarity and next actions.
2. **Parents/guardians** — need understandable guidance and confidence that they are helping the learner make an informed choice.

### Secondary

3. **Teachers/career guidance staff** — can use Pathways as a structured support tool and later provide optional professional assistance.
4. **Schools** — can use Pathways to help learners understand programmes and later receive appropriate, non-pay-to-rank learner interest.

## 6. How the Service Will Be Discovered

Pathways must not depend on users already knowing VibeSchool. It should be deliberately designed for discovery.

### Priority 1 — Search / intent discovery

Build public, indexable, useful pages around real questions learners and parents ask, for example:

- Which senior school pathway should I choose?
- STEM vs Social Sciences vs Arts and Sports pathways.
- What subjects do I need for a career?
- Which schools offer a particular pathway or subject combination?
- What can I do with my strengths/interests/results?
- How does senior school selection work?

These pages should lead naturally into the free Pathways experience.

### Priority 2 — Teacher-led distribution

Teachers are trusted distribution nodes. Provide simple tools for a teacher to:

- share the Pathways link or QR code;
- guide a class through discovery;
- invite parents to review a learner's pathway;
- share a learner-safe result or next-step link where appropriate.

The teacher should not need to sell VibeSchool. The free service should be useful enough to recommend.

### Priority 3 — Parent sharing / WhatsApp

Kenyan parent communication is highly social and mobile. Every appropriate Pathways result should have a safe, concise share route such as:

- share result summary;
- share “explore this pathway” link;
- invite parent/guardian;
- invite learner;
- share via WhatsApp or copy link.

Shared links must not expose private learner data.

### Priority 4 — Social education content

Create short, problem-led content rather than generic product adverts:

- “Your child says they want STEM. What should you check first?”
- “A pathway is not the same as a career.”
- “Three mistakes families make when choosing subjects.”
- “What happens if a learner likes Biology but struggles with Mathematics?”

TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook content should point to the free pathway tool as the next action.

### Priority 5 — School distribution

Offer schools a free pathway-discovery resource that can be used during:

- parent meetings;
- career days;
- transition counselling;
- subject selection;
- admissions/information sessions.

School participation must not distort pathway recommendations.

### Priority 6 — Referral loop

Design Pathways so a satisfied learner or parent can naturally bring another user in. The referral loop should be based on useful sharing, not spam incentives.

## 7. Acquisition Funnel

The intended customer journey is:

**Discovery channel → public pathway entry → meaningful result → free sign-in → saved pathway → personalized next steps → continued VibeSchool use → optional assistance/services**

### Stage A — Public acquisition

A learner or parent arrives from search, social media, a teacher, a school, WhatsApp, a shared pathway link or another campaign and begins pathway discovery without paying.

The first screen should communicate value, not demand registration.

### Stage B — Value before authentication

The visitor completes enough discovery to receive a useful preliminary result. The experience must demonstrate that VibeSchool understands the learner's direction before asking for an account.

### Stage C — Sign-in value moment

Authentication is requested when there is a clear reason to preserve or deepen the experience, such as:

- view the complete personalized pathway;
- save the pathway;
- receive recommended subjects or next steps;
- track progress over time;
- connect the learner and parent experience;
- continue personalized learning inside VibeSchool.

The sign-in message should explain the benefit. It must not falsely imply that the user is being charged.

### Stage D — Activation

A successful sign-in is not itself the business outcome. The newly registered user should immediately continue from the pathway context that caused the registration.

Do not discard answers, restart the assessment or send the user to an unrelated generic dashboard.

The first-session activation target is that the user saves or adopts the pathway and performs at least one meaningful next action.

### Stage E — Retention

The pathway becomes a persistent part of the learner journey rather than a one-time quiz. Future product surfaces can connect it to goals, subjects, competencies, learning activity, assessment evidence, schools and careers.

### Stage F — Optional monetization

Only after free value and trust exist should VibeSchool introduce optional paid services such as teacher questions, parent/learner consultations or other professional assistance.

Pricing and revenue share are hypotheses to validate, not fixed strategy. Low-cost entry points such as KES 50 may be tested, but no price is established by this document.

## 8. Product Architecture Direction

The desired product flow is:

**Public Pathways → Anonymous Session → Preliminary Result → Authentication Continuation → Persistent Pathway Profile → Next Actions → Learning/Progress → Parent/Teacher/School Support**

Important architecture principles:

- anonymous work should be recoverable after authentication where safe and legally appropriate;
- authentication must not destroy context;
- pathway recommendations must be explainable enough for learner/parent trust;
- recommendations and commercial offers must remain separate concerns;
- school visibility must not become hidden pay-to-rank;
- child and learner privacy constraints override growth optimization.

## 9. Economic Principle

VibeSchool should not monetize basic access to opportunity discovery.

It can monetize **additional assistance, professional expertise, convenience and services**.

This creates aligned value:

- **Learners** receive accessible direction and continuity.
- **Parents** receive understandable guidance and can purchase human help only when they want it.
- **Teachers** can earn from verified professional assistance rather than pathway access being locked behind a fee.
- **Schools** can receive better-informed prospective learners and, later, relevant service opportunities.
- **VibeSchool** acquires users through a useful free service and can build sustainable revenue around optional higher-value services.

## 10. Authentication Contract

The acquisition strategy depends on authentication being a continuation mechanism rather than a barrier.

Required product rules:

1. Do not require payment to discover a pathway.
2. Do not require authentication before the visitor has had a reasonable opportunity to understand the product's value, unless safety, privacy or legal requirements make authentication necessary.
3. Preserve anonymous pathway state through sign-up/sign-in where technically and legally appropriate.
4. After authentication, return the user to the pathway journey and restore the relevant state.
5. Clearly state why sign-in is useful.
6. Do not use deceptive scarcity, false completion claims or dark patterns to obtain registration.
7. Treat learner data, especially child data, according to VibeSchool privacy and safeguarding requirements.

## 11. Measurement Contract

Pathways becomes a customer-acquisition engine only if the funnel is measurable.

At minimum, product analytics should distinguish:

- acquisition source/campaign where privacy-safe;
- pathway landing;
- pathway start;
- meaningful discovery progress;
- preliminary result reached;
- sign-in prompt shown;
- sign-in/sign-up started;
- authentication completed;
- pathway restored after authentication;
- full result viewed;
- pathway saved/adopted;
- first meaningful next action;
- return usage;
- share/referral action;
- optional assistance viewed;
- optional assistance requested;
- optional paid transaction completed, when monetization is introduced.

Metrics must be privacy-conscious and should not collect unnecessary sensitive learner information merely for marketing attribution.

### North-star metric

**Activated Pathway Users** — registered users who successfully preserve/adopt a pathway and complete at least one meaningful next action.

### Supporting metrics

- Visitor → Pathway Start rate
- Pathway Start → Preliminary Result rate
- Result → Authentication Start rate
- Authentication Start → Authentication Complete rate
- Authentication Complete → Pathway Restored rate
- Restored → Activated Pathway User rate
- 7-day and 30-day return rate
- Useful share/referral rate
- Teacher/school referral contribution
- Cost per Activated Pathway User, when paid acquisition is tested

The team should optimize for **activated and retained users**, not raw account creation alone.

## 12. Priority Model

### P0 — Trustworthy free acquisition loop

Must exist before scaling traffic:

- pathway entry works without payment;
- useful value exists before sign-in;
- sign-in trigger occurs at the value moment;
- anonymous state survives authentication;
- user returns to the pathway rather than a generic dashboard;
- core funnel analytics are captured;
- privacy/safeguarding contract is upheld;
- recommendations are not commercially biased.

### P1 — Discovery distribution

After P0 is reliable:

- public search-intent pages;
- shareable/WhatsApp-safe links;
- teacher referral/QR flow;
- school/career-guidance distribution kit;
- social content landing routes;
- attribution that distinguishes sources without unnecessary tracking.

### P2 — Retention and pathway continuity

- saved pathway profile;
- next-step checklist/action plan;
- parent/learner connection where appropriate;
- progress and subject links;
- reminders/return hooks based on real learner tasks;
- pathway re-evaluation when evidence changes.

### P3 — Optional human assistance marketplace

Only after acquisition and retention evidence exists:

- verified teacher assistance;
- service definitions and SLAs;
- safeguarding boundaries;
- transparent pricing;
- payment/reconciliation/refund contracts;
- teacher earnings and platform fee;
- ratings/quality evidence without popularity gaming.

### P4 — School services and ecosystem expansion

- school pathway information;
- programme/subject availability;
- school guidance tools;
- appropriate learner interest signals;
- school services that do not compromise recommendation neutrality.

## 13. Roadmap

### Phase 0 — Baseline and audit

**Goal:** know exactly what exists before changing product behavior.

Deliverables:

- map current Pathways routes/components/services/data;
- map existing auth continuation behavior;
- identify current anonymous/authenticated boundaries;
- inventory analytics/events;
- identify safeguarding and privacy dependencies;
- establish current conversion baseline where data exists.

**Exit gate:** current-state architecture and gaps are documented with no major unknowns blocking implementation.

### Phase 1 — Free-to-sign-in acquisition MVP

**Goal:** create one complete, trustworthy acquisition path.

Deliverables:

- free public entry;
- meaningful preliminary result;
- contextual sign-in trigger;
- state preservation through auth;
- exact return-to-pathway continuation;
- saved/adopted pathway;
- first meaningful next action;
- acquisition/activation analytics.

**Exit gate:** end-to-end test passes from anonymous visitor through activated registered user without losing pathway state.

### Phase 2 — Discovery engine

**Goal:** make Pathways discoverable outside the existing VibeSchool audience.

Deliverables:

- SEO/search-intent landing pages;
- teacher sharing and QR flow;
- WhatsApp/share-safe result entry points;
- campaign/social landing routes;
- school guidance distribution assets;
- source attribution.

**Exit gate:** at least three distinct acquisition channels can be measured separately through activation.

### Phase 3 — Retention engine

**Goal:** turn acquired users into ongoing VibeSchool users.

Deliverables:

- persistent pathway profile;
- next-step plan;
- subject/learning connections;
- progress evidence;
- parent/learner support flow;
- useful return triggers.

**Exit gate:** retention can be measured and users have a clear reason to return beyond rereading their result.

### Phase 4 — Assistance pilot

**Goal:** test whether families voluntarily pay for additional human support.

Deliverables:

- small verified-teacher pilot;
- tightly defined services;
- transparent price experiments;
- teacher payout/revenue-share contract;
- safeguarding, escalation, refund and dispute handling;
- assistance outcome measurement.

**Exit gate:** paid assistance demonstrates user value, teacher value and operational safety before broader rollout.

### Phase 5 — School ecosystem

**Goal:** make Pathways useful to schools without corrupting learner-first recommendations.

Deliverables:

- school programme/pathway information;
- verified school data contracts;
- school-facing guidance workflows;
- privacy-safe learner interest signals;
- transparent commercial boundaries.

## 14. Execution Order

Work must proceed in this order unless evidence justifies a documented change:

1. Audit the current implementation.
2. Freeze the free-core and sign-in contract.
3. Define the canonical pathway state and persistence model.
4. Repair/implement anonymous → auth continuation.
5. Implement funnel instrumentation.
6. Implement and verify activation behavior.
7. Run usability/adversarial tests around sign-in, state loss and privacy.
8. Establish baseline funnel metrics.
9. Add discovery channels one by one.
10. Measure channel quality by activated/retained users, not visits.
11. Build retention surfaces.
12. Test optional teacher assistance only after the free acquisition loop is reliable.
13. Expand toward school services only after recommendation neutrality and data governance are protected.

## 15. Execution Rules

- Branch from `main` for each implementation unit.
- Keep strategy/documentation work separate from unrelated Worker Engine or infrastructure work.
- Do not merge implementation until relevant TypeScript, lint, build, tests and product-specific acceptance checks pass.
- Do not weaken auth, safeguarding, privacy or recommendation integrity to increase conversion.
- Do not add payment into the free pathway core.
- Do not optimize sign-up rate at the expense of activation/retention.
- Document every material strategy change in this file or its execution companion.
- A future chat/agent must inspect the repository state before assuming a roadmap item is complete.

## 16. Guardrails

The acquisition strategy must not evolve into a coercive registration or payment funnel.

- Free must mean genuinely usable for the defined free pathway core.
- Registration should unlock continuity and personalization, not manufacture artificial deprivation.
- Paid teacher assistance must remain optional.
- Teacher compensation, platform fees, refunds, disputes and safeguarding require explicit contracts before marketplace activation.
- School visibility or recommendations must not silently become pay-to-rank without explicit governance and disclosure.
- Pathway recommendations should be based on learner-relevant evidence, not monetization value to VibeSchool.

## 17. Initial Product Hypothesis

> Giving learners and parents useful pathway discovery for free, then requesting free VibeSchool registration at the moment they want to save, deepen or act on the result, will acquire higher-intent users more effectively than charging for pathway selection or requiring registration at entry.

This hypothesis must be validated with real funnel data.

## 18. Strategic Success Definition

Pathways is successful as an acquisition service when VibeSchool can demonstrate:

1. people discover it without already being VibeSchool users;
2. they receive meaningful free value;
3. a measurable proportion voluntarily create/sign into a VibeSchool account;
4. their pathway context survives the transition;
5. they take a meaningful next action;
6. a measurable proportion return;
7. referrals/distribution create additional qualified users;
8. optional monetization, when introduced, adds value without damaging the free core.

## 19. North-Star Direction

Pathways should become **VibeSchool's front door**: a useful public service that turns anonymous educational uncertainty into an ongoing learner journey.

The long-term loop is:

**Free discovery → trust → free account → persistent pathway → learning action → progress evidence → appropriate human/school support → better outcomes → referrals and return use.**

Revenue should be built around additional value created after trust, not around blocking the learner's first opportunity to understand their future.
