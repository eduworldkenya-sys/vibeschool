# VibeSchool OS — Product and Architecture Vision

**Authority:** `docs/VIBESCHOOL_MISSION_VISION.md` is the canonical company-level mission and vision.

VibeSchool is the Education Operating System that turns curriculum into coordinated teaching, teaching into evidence, evidence into understanding, and understanding into better decisions for every learner.

The platform connects Learner OS, Teacher OS, Parent OS, School OS, Publisher capabilities, VibeLearn, VibeTextbook, teaching objects, derived learning assets and VibeTwin around shared educational authority and evidence.

## North-star learning loop

`Authorized learning source → learner context → teaching/learning action → validated evidence → understanding → responsible next decision → improved learning`

AI may assist individual stages, but it must not silently invent educational facts or bypass authoritative system state.

## Permanent principles

1. One authoritative object per core concept.
2. UI consumes domain authority; it does not become an alternative source of truth.
3. AI assists people but does not fabricate educational reality.
4. Learner insights must be traceable to appropriate evidence.
5. Curriculum adaptation may change representation, support, pacing and intervention without silently changing expectations.
6. Security, authorization, privacy and provenance are architectural requirements.
7. Android, low-bandwidth, offline and printable workflows are first-class requirements.
8. Production is a release boundary: accumulate, verify and publish as a consolidated release.

## Architectural success

The platform should reliably answer what a learner should learn, what was taught, what evidence exists, what the evidence supports, who is responsible for the next decision, what should happen next, why, and what authority prevents the system from overstepping.
