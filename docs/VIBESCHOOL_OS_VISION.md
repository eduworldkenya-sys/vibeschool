This document is the authoritative long-term product vision for Vibeschool.

## Core statement

Vibeschool is the Education Operating System that turns curriculum into
coordinated teaching, teaching into evidence, evidence into understanding,
and understanding into better decisions for every learner.

## VibeTwin

VibeTwin is the context-aware intelligence embedded throughout Vibeschool.
It interprets trusted educational evidence, recommends the next responsible
action and supports teachers, learners, parents, schools and publishers.

## Permanent implementation principles

1. Design systems, not isolated pages.
2. One authoritative object per concept.
3. Teaching occurrences represent exact dated lessons.
4. UI components consume shared domain authority.
5. AI supports teachers but does not fabricate educational reality.
6. Every learner insight must be traceable to evidence.
7. Curriculum alignment is mandatory.
8. Android, low-bandwidth, offline and printable workflows are first-class.
9. Do not create parallel architectures.
10. Investigate, produce evidence, implement, verify and document every fix.
