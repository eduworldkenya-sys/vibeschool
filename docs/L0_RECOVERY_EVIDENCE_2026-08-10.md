Every inspected `hq_*` table currently has RLS enabled. However, most do not contain `school_id`.

### Critical architectural interpretation

Do **not** interpret this finding as permission to add `school_id` to every HQ table.

The frozen contract says `school_id` is mandatory on **security-relevant school-scoped contracts**. Some HQ tables are clearly platform-global control-plane objects (for example, a singleton workforce engine contract, global job/role catalogs, or owner-only governance state). Others may represent school-scoped operational data.

Therefore L0/L1 must classify each existing HQ object as:

1. **Platform-global / owner control-plane** — no school dimension by design, with explicit owner/service boundary; or
2. **School-scoped operational** — must carry and enforce `school_id`; or
3. **Derived/bridge** — inherits scope from an authoritative parent and must enforce that relationship at the boundary.

This classification is now a required part of the HQ contract mapping.
