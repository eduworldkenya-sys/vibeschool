# World Wars Curriculum Authority Cross-check v1

Status: QA evidence; NOT publication authority seal.

## Cross-check result
Multiple independently hosted reproductions of the Kenya History & Government Form IV syllabus agree on the following hierarchy and wording:

### 24.0.0 WORLD WARS
### 24.1.0 Specific Objectives
(a) explain the causes of the First and the Second World Wars;
(b) describe the course of the First and the Second World Wars;
(c) discuss the results of the First and the Second World Wars;
(d) explain the reasons for the formation of the League of Nations;
(e) describe the organisation of the League of Nations;
(f) analyse the performance of the League of Nations.

### 24.2.0 Content
- 24.2.1 The First World War (1914–1918): Causes, Course and Results.
- 24.2.2 The League of Nations: Formation, Organization, Performance.
- 24.2.3 The Second World War (1939–1945): Causes, Course and Results.

## Mapping verdict
The branch's nine learner/content leaves map exactly to the three official content sections:

1. 24.2.1(a) WWI Causes → objective 24.1.0(a)
2. 24.2.1(b) WWI Course → objective 24.1.0(b)
3. 24.2.1(c) WWI Results → objective 24.1.0(c)
4. 24.2.2(a) League Formation → objective 24.1.0(d)
5. 24.2.2(b) League Organisation → objective 24.1.0(e)
6. 24.2.2(c) League Performance → objective 24.1.0(f)
7. 24.2.3(a) WWII Causes → objective 24.1.0(a)
8. 24.2.3(b) WWII Course → objective 24.1.0(b)
9. 24.2.3(c) WWII Results → objective 24.1.0(c)

The reuse of objectives (a)–(c) across WWI and WWII is intentional in the syllabus: each objective names both wars while content separates them.
