### Fact Checker — PASS WITH REPAIRS
High-confidence anchors verified against authoritative institutional sources:
- League: Åland Islands successful intervention (1921); Manchuria (1931) and Abyssinia/Ethiopia (1935) failures damaged collective security; Germany withdrew in 1933; World Disarmament Conference failure weakened the League.
- League organs: Assembly/Council/Secretariat framework and PCIJ/ILO institutional roles are supported by UN Geneva archival history.
- WWII: Poland 1 Sep 1939; Britain/France declarations 3 Sep; Soviet entry into Poland 17 Sep; Pearl Harbor 7 Dec 1941; US declaration 8 Dec; Midway June 1942; El Alamein Oct 1942; Normandy 6 Jun 1944 are consistent with USHMM chronology.
- Kenya/East Africa WWII: King's African Rifles recruitment/training in Kenya and service in defence of Kenya, Abyssinia/Ethiopia, Madagascar and Burma are supported by National Army Museum records.

Repairs/constraints:
- avoid unsupported exact casualty figures unless sourced by range/method;
- keep contested causal claims qualified (Versailles, appeasement, atomic-bomb surrender causation);
- do not invent Kenya-local anecdotes or precise manpower numbers without a source locator;
- distinguish League core organs from associated/specialised bodies accurately;
- do not imply Åland/Upper Silesia successes prove equal capacity against major-power aggression.
