# VibeSchool Education Knowledge Authority

## Objective

VibeSchool should become a trusted public explanation layer for Kenyan education without becoming a high-volume unsourced SEO publisher.

The content system starts with **provenance**, then expands coverage.

## Content contract

Every section is one of three types:

- **Fact** — attributed to a current source authority.
- **Guidance** — a practical recommendation that helps someone act on facts but is not presented as an official rule.
- **Boundary** — a warning where concepts can be dangerously or commercially misleading if collapsed together.

Every section in V1 has one or more source IDs. Sources include authority, title, URL, source type and last verification date.

## V1 authority set

- Kenya Ministry of Education Grade 10 School & Pathway Selection System.
- Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development Grade 10 curriculum designs.
- Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development approved CBC course-materials register.
- Kenya National Examinations Council Competency Based Assessment Portal.

## Initial high-intent questions

1. What is Grade 10 in Kenya and what changes at Senior School?
2. What are the Senior School pathways?
3. How should a learner think about Grade 10 subject combinations?
4. How should a family choose a Senior School without confusing preference with placement?

These are a foundation, not the target coverage ceiling.

## Expansion rule

Future topics should be added in clusters only after their source set is defined. Priority clusters:

- Grade 10 subjects and curriculum designs;
- pathway and career questions;
- school-selection and school-offering questions;
- CBE assessment and evidence;
- parent explanations;
- teacher planning / schemes / assessment practice;
- approved learning resources and provenance.

Do not manufacture hundreds of near-duplicate pages. One canonical source-backed answer should own each important question, with product experiences linked where they add real value.

## Staleness rule

A page can remain public after its verification date, but VibeSchool should not silently describe it as newly verified. High-consequence facts — pathway structure, combinations, school offerings, assessment rules and approved materials — should be rechecked when the authority changes its source or when a new academic transition cycle begins.

## Product connection

Knowledge is not a separate blog strategy. It should route useful intent into the product:

**Question → authoritative explanation → inspect evidence → explore Pathways / school discovery / curriculum → take the next legitimate action.**
