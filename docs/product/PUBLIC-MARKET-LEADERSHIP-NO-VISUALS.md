# Public Market Leadership — No-Visual Strategy

Status: implementation contract
Date: 2026-08-17

## Objective

Make VibeSchool's public experience commercially stronger without depending on product screenshots, fabricated customer evidence, or visual spectacle.

The site should win through useful interaction, educational intelligence, transparency, trust, and conversion quality.

## Competitive premise

Competitors can win with screenshots, long module lists, low published prices, and established adoption proof. VibeSchool should not imitate those advantages before it has equivalent evidence.

VibeSchool's defensible public proposition is connected educational continuity:

Curriculum → Scheme → Lesson → Teaching → Learning → Evidence → Assessment → Understanding → Next action.

## V1 work package

### 1. School Readiness Assessment

Replace the passive readiness questions with an interactive diagnostic. It must:
- ask concise operational/learning questions;
- score locally in the browser without collecting learner data;
- return a useful readiness band and gap categories;
- provide recommendations mapped to VibeSchool capabilities;
- clearly state that the score is a self-assessment, not an audit or certification;
- offer an institution CTA after delivering value.

### 2. Role journey selector

Let visitors identify as Learner, Teacher, Parent/Family, or School Leader and immediately see the most relevant VibeSchool value chain and destination. Do not hide the general homepage from users who do not select a role.

### 3. Connected Education Explorer

Create an accessible interactive explanation of the canonical learning chain. Each stage must explain:
- what enters the stage;
- what becomes known or actionable;
- who benefits;
- what connects to the next stage.

This is an explanatory model, not a claim that every workflow has been production-certified.

### 4. Why VibeSchool

Compare approaches rather than named competitors:
- disconnected curriculum documents vs connected curriculum context;
- isolated lesson plans vs teaching continuity;
- marks-only reporting vs evidence-informed understanding;
- end-term-only visibility vs continuing progress context;
- generic all-user portal vs role-appropriate experiences;
- separate career guidance vs connected Pathways context.

Avoid unverifiable superiority language.

### 5. Public capability status

Publish a transparent vocabulary for product claims:
- Available — implemented public/product capability with an appropriate route;
- Validation — implemented or integrated capability still undergoing operational/pilot proof;
- Planned — direction only, not sold as available.

Do not expose security-sensitive implementation details.

### 6. School objection handling

Answer the questions a Kenyan school leader needs before contacting sales: curriculum fit, onboarding, privacy/access, mobile use, data migration expectations, support, parent/learner participation, evidence boundaries, and pricing availability. Never invent contractual commitments.

### 7. Discoverability

Ensure the public site has strong metadata, crawlable semantic content, sitemap/robots correctness, canonical routes, internal links, and structured data where truthful. Do not use keyword stuffing or competitor names in public SEO copy.

### 8. Conversion instrumentation

Measure role selection, readiness start/completion, readiness band, explorer interaction, institution intent and contact intent without placing sensitive educational data into analytics payloads.

## Explicit non-goals

- no screenshots required for this package;
- no fake interactive backend;
- no fake schools, testimonials, metrics or adoption figures;
- no competitor-bashing;
- no unapproved pricing;
- no production Supabase mutations;
- no weakening authentication or privacy boundaries;
- no claim that a self-assessment is professional certification.

## Acceptance

The exact branch head must pass TypeScript/production build, public browser/mobile, public trust, entry architecture and any new deterministic regression checks introduced by this package before merge.
