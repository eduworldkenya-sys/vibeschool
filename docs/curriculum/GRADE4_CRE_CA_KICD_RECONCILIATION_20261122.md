## Verified discrepancies and open checks
1. **Creative Arts optional numbering:** KICD summary pp. x–xi calls both alternative Swimming and Indigenous Kenyan Games **2.5**, following Descant Recorder also numbered **2.5**. Current VibeSchool records call the alternatives **2.6**. Treat the duplicate KICD source numbering as an edition/source ambiguity; do not silently invent a canonical 2.6 code. Preserve alternative semantics, and reconcile against the detailed body.
2. **Creative Arts Photography:** KICD summary p. x gives **10 lessons**; prior import noted **8** in detail. Lesson count needs a source-locator-based decision, not a guess.
3. **CRE 1.1 Self-awareness:** KICD regular Grade 4 p. 1 gives **2 lessons** and four outcomes. VibeSchool has four mapped outcomes, but no authoritative per-unit lesson allocation in the imported cbc_strands fields.
4. **CRE 1.2 Thoughts and feelings:** KICD p. 1 gives **3 lessons** and four outcomes; VibeSchool has four mapped outcomes. Exact semantic correspondence remains unreviewed.
5. **CRE 1.6 Attributes of God:** KICD p. 3 gives **1 lesson** and three outcomes; VibeSchool has three mapped outcomes. Suggested lessons are not recorded in cbc_strands.
6. **CRE 6.1 Use of Social Media:** KICD p. 53 gives **2 lessons** and four outcomes; VibeSchool has four mapped outcomes. Source's appendix uses variant labels ('Social Media and Morality' and 'Appropriate use of social media'); the body controls.
7. **Creative Arts learning outcomes:** 64 are original paraphrases, **not official verbatim outcomes**; one-to-one scope, assessment verb, content constraints, and source page references have not all been certified.
8. **CRE outcomes:** 129 are owner-approved, **not independently verified**; full 129-vs-source row-by-row comparison and rights verification are still required.
9. **Teacher teaching path:** No production authenticated teacher end-to-end evidence yet; do not certify teacher visibility from public Learn deployment alone.

## Certification acceptance
For every source sub-strand, record source edition, PDF printed page, KICD code and title, suggested lessons, exact number of source outcomes, paraphrase-to-source one-to-one correspondence and semantic review, alternative-unit treatment, and independent reviewer evidence. Flag unresolved discrepancies, do not silently edit. Confirm no missing/extra units. Run guest Learn, student Learn, and authenticated teacher scheme/lesson-plan acceptance; verify production deployment. Only then mark **internally KICD-aligned certified**, never 'KICD-approved' without explicit KICD approval.

## Non-destructive decision
This audit changes no live curriculum text or status. Current records remain accessible as owner-approved or paraphrased content pending certification.
