# Grade 4 Kiswahili — KICD 2024 source index and reconciliation

Authority: **KICD** (Kiswahili, Gredi ya 4, Chapisho la Pili 2024).
Original document (remain with publisher): https://kicd.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Kiswahili-Gredi-ya-4-Revised.pdf
ISBN: 978-9914-43-068-4. Structural reference: source pages xiii–xv (index), themes pages 1–103.

## What is implemented
- 11 ordered source themes; four language strands, 52 uniquely coded sub-strands.
- 4 recommended Kiswahili periods a week in KICD's upper-primary allocation table.
- Native teacher Curriculum Map, original document link, theme selector, search, source-index headings and recommended per-unit lesson count, Scheme entry.
- Teacher Subject Hub entry. Existing teacher-owned schemes, Grade 4 English and other learning areas remain unchanged.
- No invented official learning outcomes or activities. This is **a navigational curriculum index**, not an authorized verbatim reproduction, and is not claimed as the complete authenticated KICD outcome database.
- Teacher-owned scheme records are **not** automatically created by browsing the curriculum; the teacher's school calendar and subject assignment remain authoritative for teaching schedules.

## Source discrepancy, never force-fix
KICD's index **prints 120 lessons**, but summing its 52 listed unit allocations yields **121**. Specifically the index allocates 3 to 7.2.1 while most reading entries have 2, and the source's unit 1.1.1 body also shows 4 versus the index's 2. The original KICD is the reference. VibeSchool shows the source allocation with a clear caution rather than falsely calling the inferred sum official or silently changing lesson counts. **Automatic annual Scheme pacing remains blocked until a trusted reconciliation of the official allocation**; the Scheme itself remains usable.
