## 1. Non-negotiable zero-omission inventory

For **every source document**, create an inventory of **every page and every substantive section actually present**, including front matter, source notices, main tables, page continuations and appendices. The checklist below is a baseline; an actual KICD edition may have additional or differently named sections. Add them rather than silently discard them. Mark absent elements `NOT_IN_SOURCE` only after checking relevant pages.

### A. Document authority and front matter
- Official publisher/authority, grade/stage, subject/learning area, curriculum framework.
- Full document title, release/revision/edition/date, relevant foreword/preface/acknowledgments, ISBN if given, source URL, exact artifact identifier and acquisition date.
- Copyright/reuse terms, rights decision, permission evidence if applicable.
- Contents/section order, page numbering (printed and PDF physical page index separately), errata/addenda/version conflicts.
- National Goals of Education and other foundational framework sections present.
- Level/phase (e.g., Junior School) learning outcomes, essence/rationale, subject general learning outcomes.
- Official learning-area or subject lesson allocation, duration per lesson and relevant scope/qualifications.

### B. Curriculum hierarchy
- Every official **strand**: printed code, name, order, scope.
- Every **sub-strand**: printed code, title, scope/learning focus, sequence, proposed lesson count and its source location.
- Each **specific learning outcome as a separate atomic requirement**, including its letter/code/order, action and intended observable competence.
- Every **suggested learning experience** as a separate atomic requirement, preserving skill, modality and progression.
- Every **suggested key inquiry question** as a separate atomic requirement, preserving its intended inquiry.
- All listed **core competencies**, including the contextual explanation of how developed.
- All listed **values**, including their associated learning context.
- All **Pertinent and Contemporary Issues (PCIs)**, including contextual link.
- All **links to other learning areas**, as distinct records; do not collapse them into a generic tag.
- Any other row/note/teacher guidance attached to that sub-strand, including tables continued on subsequent pages.

### C. Assessment, support and implementation
- Every applicable **assessment rubric**, criteria, indicators, level names/descriptors and scale order; separate rubric descriptors from VibeSchool-generated marking guidance.
- Suggested assessment approaches/methods, if listed.
- Suggested teaching/learning resources, technology/ICT guidance, equipment and accessibility requirements, if listed.
- Guidelines for Community Service Learning projects and other whole-subject appendices where present.
- Non-formal activities and any applicable cross-curricular implementation guidance.
- Figures, charts, diagrams, tables and their *meaning*, captions, labels, numerical values and placement. Rights to republish the visual must be evaluated separately; where not licensed, produce an original educationally equivalent visual.
- Notes, footnotes, exceptions, suggested/mandatory distinctions, source errata, cross-references and unexplained gaps.

## 6. Coverage and quality gates — fail closed

### Gate A — SOURCE_VERIFIED
- Correct KICD version, genuine original file, complete page inventory, coherent printed/PDF page mapping.
- All sections, appendices, matrices, diagrams and continuation pages listed with access evidence.
- Private verbatim rights scope and handling recorded as verified or `RIGHTS_SCOPE_UNVERIFIED`; never manufacture evidence of permission.

### Gate B — SOURCE_INVENTORY_COMPLETE
- Every official strand and sub-strand present, in correct order; all source counts reconciled.
- Count each individual outcome, experience, inquiry, competency, value, PCI, link, assessment criterion/descriptor and applicable resource/appendix item; **never count a populated field as proof all its bullets are present**.
- Detect blank extraction pages, broken tables, duplicates and inconsistent summary/detail counts; resolve or explicitly mark a conflict.

### Gate C — CANONICAL_RECONCILIATION
- Before writes, compare actual VibeSchool canonical rows to every source requirement; preserve existing identities/history.
- For the complete **declared private transcription scope**, source-exact words, punctuation, order, tables and every atomic requirement must be `EXACT_MATCH` after verified source comparison; `MISSING`, `PARTIAL`, `CONFLICT` or `NOT_CHECKED` cannot count as complete.
- Verify source locators for all items. Do not substitute previous chat, a candidate JSON, a test fixture or a remembered textbook.

### Gate D — PERSISTED_READBACK
- After authorized writes, independently re-query live canonical private rows. Verify IDs, **verbatim wording**, punctuation, counts, order, table-cell values, rubric descriptors, source pages, associations and version. Compute and review source-text diffs where extractable; manually inspect ambiguous PDF layout or images.
- Compare **source inventory count vs mapped count vs persisted verified count for EACH requirement type**. `N/N` for topics only is insufficient.
- Verify teacher Curriculum Map and relevant Scheme search, learner content where legitimately published, and linked resources separately. A GitHub merge or draft JSON alone is not a live database result.

### Gate E — INDEPENDENT_ASSURANCE_AND_RELEASE
- Obtain any required separate independent review and relevant owner/authority approval. The author cannot self-certify.
- Complete educational quality and legal/rights gates, then only the preexisting controlled publication/release workflow may expose content to users.
- Do not claim KICD endorsement, approval or official digital-edition status without documented authorization.

**Gate result values:** `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, `BLOCKED_SOURCE_ACCESS`, `BLOCKED_RIGHTS`, `BLOCKED_CURRICULUM_RECONCILIATION`, `BLOCKED_AUTHORITY`, `NOT_TESTED`. No invented 100% score. A failed gate is a **status for a clearly identified scope**, not an instruction to abandon unrelated safely completed draft work.
