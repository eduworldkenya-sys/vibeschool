## Confirmed discrepancy

The source's Grade 1 summary of strands and sub-strands appears on PDF page 12 (printed page 3). It organizes strands as Listening and Speaking, Reading, Language Use, and Writing, with **13** listed sub-strands (1, 4, 3, and 5 respectively). Term/week pacing is not established by this source.

Production `public.cbc_strands` has **12 Grade 1 English hierarchy rows**, organized instead as:

- Listening and Speaking: 3 rows
- Reading: 3 rows
- Language Structure and Use: 3 rows
- Writing: 3 rows

The 12 rows have zero source references, zero embedded learning outcomes, and no bound verified `curriculum_learning_outcomes`. The source taxonomy is not identical to these unsourced placeholder labels. Existing Grade 1 English class Scheme records for the checked school are from earlier terms and lack authoritative curriculum/outcome references. Current Term 3 has no Scheme.

**Never silently map the 12 unsourced nodes onto 13 official sub-strands or promote generated/creator-claimed outcomes as KICD verified.**

## Owner-gated recovery plan

1. Confirm any necessary rights or permissions for source storage/transcription in a commercial educational product. The KICD document carries an all-rights-reserved notice. Linking to the publicly available official document is distinct from reproducing its full contents.
2. Use the existing Curriculum Authority HQ pipeline: register the correct KICD version and scope; retain SHA-256 artifact evidence; stage exact source-backed observations with section/page locators where permitted; seal and reconcile.
3. Reconcile the Grade 1 English hierarchy **by exact canonical grade, subject, strand, sub-strand and official source version**, not fuzzy matches. Classify unmatched and conflicting nodes before owner approval.
4. Owner review and promotion must remain explicit. Do not insert outcomes directly into the operational `curriculum` pacing table or mark them verified based on an external URL alone.
5. Only after canonical outcome verification, construct a school-authorized Term 3 Scheme for the specific class, with appropriate instructional weeks, actual subject allocation, lesson sequence and teacher assignment. No national document alone determines school-specific weeks.
6. Re-test Lesson Plan and Prepare Ahead for this class, including plan readiness and dated attachment. Verify Android with an authenticated teacher and another-school denial.
