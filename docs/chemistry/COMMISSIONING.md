# Grade 10 Chemistry commissioning

Chemistry is commissioned as one bounded operator action while preserving every governed stage internally.

## Operator flow

`Commission chapter` repeatedly executes the canonical `chemistry-stage-executor` and refreshes the authoritative Laban mission dashboard after each stage.

The chain is:

Author → Quality → independent Critic → Repair when required → fresh Quality → fresh independent Critic → Human Review.

The command stops at Human Review, Converged, Blocked Readiness, Escalated, Paused, an unexpected stage, safe-posture drift, or the eight-stage safety bound.

## Resume behavior

The operator may resume both queued and already-running stage names. This is required for expired lease recovery. For example, an item left at `AUTHORING` maps back to the canonical `AUTHOR_QUEUED` claim request; `chemistry_claim_stage()` remains the authority that decides whether the existing lease is replayable or expired and must be timed out/reclaimed.

No client-side state rewrite is allowed.

## Safety boundaries

Commissioning does not enable Worker runtime, shadow runtime, scheduler, publishing, payments, autonomy, or risk. Global Stop remains ON. Every model call still passes through the lease-bound Cyborg admission/gateway and immutable evidence bridge. Human publication/release authority is not automated.

## Purpose

This turns Chemistry into the canonical commissioning pattern for future subjects: one operator command, many internally governed stages, deterministic stop conditions, resumable leases, and evidence at every boundary.
