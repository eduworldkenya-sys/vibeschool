# Grade 10 Chemistry Learning Quality Contract

This document is the readable companion to the enforced database contract in `20260823201500_content_worker_professionalization_chemistry_learning_quality_contract.sql`.

A Chemistry chapter is not complete because it is long, fluent, generated, or marked complete by its author. It is complete only when evidence demonstrates that every required curriculum outcome is taught, exemplified, experienced by the learner, practised, assessed, and supported for the teacher.

## Governed production path

`Author → Quality → Independent Critic → Repair → Fresh Quality → Fresh Critic → Human Review`

No worker may publish, self-certify, bypass Global Stop, or grant itself authority.

## Hard learning gates

Every governed chapter must demonstrate:

- curriculum outcome traceability;
- scientific correctness;
- conceptual explanation rather than definition-only summaries;
- worked or concrete examples where required;
- executable learner activities;
- guided practice;
- misconception identification and correction;
- assessment aligned to content actually taught;
- answers or marking guidance;
- complete teacher support;
- safe practical procedures when practical work exists;
- Kenyan classroom feasibility;
- independent criticism;
- fresh independent verification after repair.

## Outcome definition of done

For every required outcome, the evidence must show:

1. concept explanation;
2. worked or concrete example;
3. learner activity or experience;
4. guided practice;
5. assessment evidence;
6. teacher support;
7. expected learner evidence.

Missing one of these is a defect, not a reason to add unrelated word count.

## Practical Chemistry definition of done

Where practical work is required, the artifact must provide the objective, materials/apparatus, safety controls, executable procedure, expected observations, interpretation, relevant equation, cleanup/disposal where applicable, learner questions, and expected answers.

## Assessment rule

Each chapter targets at least six formal assessment items. Items must test taught outcomes, include correct answers or marking guidance, and provide evidence of mastery. Questions on untaught material are release blockers.

## Repair rule

Repair workers fix demonstrated defects, preserve unaffected approved material, and supply regression evidence. Repair completion is never accepted as final quality proof; fresh Quality and fresh Independent Critic stages must pass again.

## Release rule

AI workers cannot declare publication readiness. Only current evidence plus human release authority can move Chemistry beyond governed review.
