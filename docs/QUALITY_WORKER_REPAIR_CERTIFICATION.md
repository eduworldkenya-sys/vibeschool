# Quality Worker Repair & Certification Mission

## Mission
Repair `quality-worker-01` into the independent professional assurance authority for evaluating other workers. Certification is evidence-backed and must not grant runtime authority.

## Locked scope
1. Diagnose the exact current qualification failure and missing evidence.
2. Strengthen Quality-specific competencies, evidence semantics, evaluation rubric, adversarial coverage, conflict-of-interest rules, and fail-closed behavior.
3. Require reproducible findings with severity, evidence, expected/observed behavior, remediation and re-verification state.
4. Prohibit self-certification, Worker Creator certification, unsupported pass decisions, authority escalation and mutation of the worker being evaluated.
5. Exercise representative cross-worker evaluations and known-bad fixtures.
6. Record repair evidence, then fresh independent re-verification after repair.
7. Run required R1 shadow evidence without enabling autonomous runtime.
8. Re-run Global Stop and authority-separation evidence.
9. Certify only when every professional gate passes; otherwise remain NEEDS_REPAIR.
10. Merge only exact-head validated work and re-check production after merge/migration.

## Definition of done
- Quality Worker has a durable professional assurance contract.
- Independent/adversarial evaluation is reproducible and evidence-backed.
- Known-bad cases are rejected and known-good controls pass.
- Fresh re-verification follows the repair event.
- Required shadow evidence passes.
- Global Stop and authority separation remain intact.
- `quality-worker-01` is `CERTIFIED` only from legitimate evidence.
- No activation, capability grant, autonomy increase, factory enablement, heartbeat enablement, or Global Stop weakening occurs as part of certification.
